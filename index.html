Lector

Your own books, read beautifully — or read to you.

Lector is an ebook reader for Android phones and tablets, with a read-aloud voice that runs entirely on your device.

What it does

  • Reads EPUB, PDF and comics, and converts TXT, Markdown, HTML, FB2, DOCX, ODT and RTF on import.
  • Highlights, notes, bookmarks, search and a built-in dictionary.
  • Offline neural read-aloud with fine control over speed, pitch, pauses, pronunciation and what gets skipped.
  • Optional sync between your devices through a private folder in your own Google Drive.

Your data

Lector has no servers and no accounts of its own. Your books and reading data stay on your device and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive. Read the privacy policy.
