Lector

Your own books, read beautifully — or read to you.

Lector is an ebook reader for Android phones and tablets, with a read-aloud voice that runs entirely on your device.

Download

Lector is for Android 10 or newer, installed from the APK file (it isn't on the Play Store).

  1. On your phone, open the latest release and tap the Lector-….apk file.
  2. Open the download. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Optional: in Lector, Settings → Connect Google Drive, then long-press any book and choose Sync this book to have it on all your devices. Books stay on the device you added them to until you do.

Lector tells you when a newer version is out; installing it keeps your books and settings.

What it does

  • Reads EPUB, PDF and comics, and converts TXT, Markdown, HTML, FB2, DOCX, ODT and RTF on import.
  • Highlights, notes, bookmarks, search and a built-in dictionary.
  • Offline neural read-aloud with fine control over speed, pitch, pauses, pronunciation and what gets skipped.
  • Optional sync of the books you choose, between your devices, through a private folder in your own Google Drive.

Your data

Lector has no servers and no accounts of its own. Your books and reading data stay on your device and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive. Read the privacy policy.
